# Rivtor Launch Film Storyboard

## Vision

Show Rivtor as a serious operating system for autonomous companies: directive in, decision made, governed execution, measurable outcome.

## Narrative Arc (19s)

1. **Intent (0:00-0:03)**
   - Founder directive appears with typewriter rhythm.
   - Rivtor mark and `decision_id` establish auditability from frame one.

2. **Coordination (0:03-0:06)**
   - Rivtor 01 hub routes intent across C-suite agent nodes.
   - Data rails animate outward to show parallel delegation.

3. **Decision Quality (0:06-0:09)**
   - Taste pipeline with explicit stages and option cards.
   - Winner card locks in after simulation and critique.

4. **Control Layer (0:09-0:12)**
   - Governance panel checks authority, budget, reversibility, and risk.
   - Approval stamp lands only after checks pass.

5. **Execution (0:12-0:15)**
   - DAG tasks move from pending to running to done.
   - Live event rail confirms deterministic operational flow.

6. **Integration Surface (0:15-0:17)**
   - Real partner logos in production-grid treatment.
   - Each cell shows operational status, not decorative branding.

7. **Business Outcome (0:17-0:19)**
   - Outcome card reports conversion uplift and reliability metrics.
   - Learning loop text closes with Rivtor mark lockup.

## Motion Language

- TransitionSeries crossfades with short overlap to feel cinematic, not slideshow.
- Spring-driven entrances for cards and nodes, linear sweeps for data rails.
- Dense but clean typography hierarchy (`Inter Tight` + `JetBrains Mono`).

## Visual Rules

- Palette: deep graphite base, violet authority, cyan system activity.
- Hairline frames, projection-grid background, subtle radial light.
- Every scene includes either state change, status confirmation, or measurable result.
